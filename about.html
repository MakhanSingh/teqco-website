Local know-how, serious stock

Teqco is the comprehensive retail outlet for the rural, trade and handyman market on the corner of Bathurst and Denison Streets, Condobolin.

Our story

Same corner, same team, same range

Teqco has taken over CS Steel and carries on everything the district relied on it for: a vast selection of steel section, sheet and mesh, hand and power tools, fasteners, hardware and the general merchandise every rural job needs.

We serve farmers, tradespeople and handymen across Condobolin and the Lachlan Valley. Whether you are fencing a paddock, fitting out a shed or fixing something at home, we have the stock and the know-how to get you sorted quickly, with fast, friendly service from people who know the land.

What we do

More than a shop counter

Stock is only half of it. We cut, fold, source and advise, so you leave with the right thing for the job.

Steel supply

RHS, SHS, pipe, angle, flat bar, sheet, mesh and roofing from Steeline, Lysaght and Arc Metalcentre, cut to length on request.

Cutting and folding
