About Teqco

Local knowledge. A good stock. Friendly service.

Based on the corner of Bathurst and Denison Streets in Condobolin, Teqco supplies steel, tools, hardware and much more to farmers, tradies and locals across the district.

Formerly CS Steel, Teqco continues to provide a broad range of products and practical services for jobs on the farm, at work and around the home. Pop in, tell us what you're working on and we'll do our best to point you in the right direction.

  • A wide stock under one roof – Steel, tools, fasteners, hardware and everyday essentials for work and around the property.
  • Practical, local know-how – Friendly service to help you find the right gear for the job.
  • Serving Condobolin and the district – Your local supplier for rural, trade and home projects.

FAQ

Frequently asked

What are your trading hours?

We are open Monday to Friday from 7am to 5pm. We are closed on weekends and public holidays.

What payment options do you accept?

All major credit cards and cash. Trade accounts are available on request for regular customers.

Where are you located?

22 Bathurst Street, Condobolin NSW 2877, on the corner of Bathurst and Denison Streets.

Is Teqco the same business as CS Steel?

Yes. Teqco has taken over CS Steel and continues to offer the same products and services from the same location.
