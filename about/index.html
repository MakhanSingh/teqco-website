Our story

A local name for the jobs that matter

Located on the corner of Bathurst and Denison Streets in Condobolin, Teqco supplies farmers, tradespeople and handymen across the Lachlan Valley with the steel, tools and hardware they need to get the job done.

Previously CS Steel, Teqco continues to supply a wide range of steel sections, sheet metal, mesh, hand and power tools, fasteners and general hardware. With practical advice and friendly service, we’re here to help you find the right gear for the job, big or small.

What we do

More than just supplies

From steel and hardware to custom sheet metal work, we offer practical solutions and helpful advice to keep your projects moving.

Steel supply

From RHS and SHS to pipe, angle, flat bar, sheet metal, mesh and roofing, we stock a wide stock of steel products for farm, building and workshop projects. Cut-to-length options are available on selected products.

Cutting and folding

Need sheet metal cut or folded to size? We offer in-store guillotine cutting and folding for sheet metal up to 3mm thick. Get in touch to discuss your measurements and requirements.
